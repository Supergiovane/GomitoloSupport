Privacy policy

Gomitolo for iPhone · Updated 10 November 2026

No data collected

Gomitolo does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, advertising, tracking or analytics, and it does not connect to any server of the developer.

Saved on your iPhone

Your stars, the levels you have reached and your settings (coat colour, sound, music and vibration) are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks every level is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Italiano

Gomitolo non raccoglie, non conserva e non condivide alcun dato personale: niente account, pubblicità, tracciamento o statistiche. Stelle, livelli raggiunti e impostazioni restano solo sul tuo iPhone. L'acquisto unico è gestito interamente da Apple.
