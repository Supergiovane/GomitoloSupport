A yarn, a cat, a cosy bed.

Support for Gomitolo for iPhone, by Massimo Saccani.

Gomitolo is a gentle game: draw a path of yarn with your finger and a fluffy Siberian cat follows it, gathering treats and hearts, meeting butterflies, birds and friends, all the way to its bed. Its day goes from home to the garden, into the woods and back home at night.

How to play

  • Touch the cat and draw the yarn to its bed, then tap Go.
  • Pick up hearts and treats on the way, and use as little yarn as you can, for three stars.
  • In the side-view levels, draw the yarn from shelf to shelf or from branch to branch: the cat leaps one at a time.
  • Some treats are too far away: sometimes the best choice is to leave them and head to bed.

Free levels and the full game

The first three levels are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks every level, for ever: there are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing else to buy. If you reinstall the app or change iPhone, open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchase.

Contact support

For questions or problems, write to maxsupergiovane@icloud.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number.
